Five new ways to check alignment:

- **Pixel loupe:** 4×, 8×, and 16× magnification with a backing-pixel grid and coordinates. Toggle with Control–Option–M. Requires Screen Recording permission only when you use it; capture stays local, with no audio, saved images, or uploads.
- **Move several guides together:** Shift-click or drag a selection rectangle in Edit lines, then drag or nudge the selection. Style, duplicate, delete, and undo also work on the selected guides.
- **Follow a window:** select guides, choose Attach…, and click a window. Guides follow its movement, hide when it is minimized or on another Space, and detach when it closes. Attachments last for the current session.
- **High contrast:** a black-and-white outline makes guides visible over both light and dark content, without changing measured positions.
- **Your shortcuts:** use the gear button or Keyboard Shortcuts… to record the four global combinations. Conflict detection, cancel, and restore defaults are included.

Existing guides and settings are preserved. The controls scroll on smaller screens. macOS 13 or later; universal Apple Silicon and Intel build. This build is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarized.
